SRM University Library Policy

1. Library Hours:
   - Monday to Friday: 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM
   - Saturday: 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM
   - Sunday: 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM

2. Borrowing Rules:
   - Students can borrow up to 5 books at a time
   - Faculty can borrow up to 10 books at a time
   - Books can be borrowed for 14 days
   - Renewal is allowed for 7 additional days

3. Late Return Policy:
   - Fine of Rs. 5 per day for each overdue book
   - Maximum fine of Rs. 100 per book
   - Students with overdue books cannot borrow new books

4. Library Resources:
   - Over 50,000 books in various subjects
   - Digital library with e-books and journals
   - Computer lab with internet access
   - Study rooms for group discussions

5. Contact Information:
   - Phone: +91-44-2741-7000
   - Email: library@srmuniv.ac.in
   - Location: Ground Floor, Main Building
